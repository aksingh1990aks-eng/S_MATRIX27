Symposium on Millimeter-wave AnTenna and RF Integrated Circuits and Systems

Organized by: Anusandhan National Research Foundation, New Delhi & IEEE MTT-S SBC, Dept of ECE, GGV

January 22-23, 2027 | Bilaspur, India

About the Symposium

Primary Theme: "From Millimeter-Wave Research to Real-World Innovation"
Bridging Research, Indigenous Technology Development and Industrial Realization

S-MATRICS-2027 focuses on transforming advances in millimeter-wave technology into practical, reliable, and indigenous solutions for emerging applications. The symposium brings together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, startups, students, and scientists from national R&D organizations to exchange ideas in millimeter-wave antennas, RF circuits, RFIC/MMIC, advanced packaging, and integrated systems.

Emphasis will be placed on bridging fundamental research with technology development, prototyping, characterization, manufacturing, and industrial deployment. The symposium aims to encourage interdisciplinary collaboration and identify new opportunities in 5G/6G, satellite communication, radar, electronic warfare, aerospace, automotive sensing, and space electronics, contributing to a stronger indigenous high-frequency technology ecosystem.
